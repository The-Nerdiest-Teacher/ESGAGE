Student council

Elected students who represent their peers and get things moving at school

The council's role

The student council is the link between students and the school's administration. Its members listen to their classmates' ideas, plan theme days, dances and major events, and manage a budget for student projects.

At the end of each school year, students vote to elect the executive and the representatives of each grade for the following year. Being on the council is a great way to build leadership, public speaking and organizational skills.

  • Elections held at the end of the school year
  • Regular meetings with a staff advisor
  • An idea box open to all students

The positions

  • PresidentLeads meetings and represents students.
  • Vice-presidentSupports the president and coordinates committees.
  • TreasurerManages the budget for student activities.
  • SecretaryTakes notes and shares announcements.
  • Grade representativesSpeak for each grade.
