About our specialized programs

Gaétan-Gervais offers three enriched programs that help students stand out, dig deeper into their passions and prepare for their future, whether on the international stage, in elite sport or in technology.

International Baccalaureate

A program recognized worldwide, built on academic rigour and global citizenship

International program

International Baccalaureate (IB)

The International Baccalaureate is recognized in more than 160 countries. It prepares students for success in post-secondary studies through rigorous, intercultural learning focused on critical thinking.

  • Available from grade 9 to grade 12
  • Courses in every subject of the IB core
  • Theory of Knowledge (TOK) and the Extended Essay
  • CAS: Creativity, Activity, Service
  • A diploma recognized by universities in Canada and abroad
Grades 9 – 12 In French IB Diploma

Specialist High Skills Major

A program where students build in-depth expertise in one field (called MHS in French)

SHSM Sports

Specialist High Skills Major: Sports

SHSM Sports lets student-athletes combine excellence in sport with academic success. The program gives athletes who train at a high level the support they need to keep up with their studies.

  • A schedule adapted to training and competitions
  • Development of sport and athletic skills
  • Specialized physical education courses
  • Support in nutrition and mental preparation
  • Opportunities for university athletic recruitment
Grades 9 – 12 Elite sport SHSM certificate
SHSM ICT

Specialist High Skills Major: ICT

SHSM Information and Communications Technology trains the next generation of digital professionals. Students build advanced technical skills in programming, robotics and computer systems.

  • Programming, web and app development
  • Robotics and embedded systems (TER)
  • Computer networks and cybersecurity
  • Hands-on projects with industry partners
  • Preparation for college and university programs in IT
Grades 9 – 12 Technology SHSM certificate
